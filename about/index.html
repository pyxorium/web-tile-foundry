About the Web Tile Foundry

The Web Tile Foundry is a workshop for making web tiles. Pick a kind of tile, make it your own, and publish it to your atproto account.

What's a web tile?

A web tile is a tiny app: a little walking sprite, a stained glass lantern you can roll, a mixtape you can play, a zine you can flip through. Many web tiles include interactive features. Everything a tile needs is packed into one bundle and stored in your own atproto repo, the same account you use for Bluesky and other apps in the Atmosphere. Because it lives with you rather than on someone else's server, any site that understands web tiles can show it. You can browse them on appmosphe.re, or embed them in your own site.

What you can make

  • Sprite Walker: your rpg.actor sprite, out for a walk.
  • Glass Lantern: a stained glass die, lit from inside, to turn and roll.
  • Coaster Carnival: a roller coaster to ride, with your sprite in the front row.
  • Mixtape C60: your plyr.fm songs on a cassette, Side A and Side B.
  • Zine Scene: a little eight-page zine of pictures, words, and more. Pages can bring in your photos from Grain, books from Bookhive, activities from Treeline, sound from plyr.fm, and your other tiles.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your atproto handle.
  2. Pick a tile type and fill it in. The preview updates as you go.
  3. Review the card as feeds will show it, then publish.

You get a link to share and the tile's address for embedding it elsewhere.

Why web tiles?

Much of what people make online lives on a few big platforms, on their servers and under their rules. Web tiles point another way: small, personal, sometimes odd things, kept in the accounts of the people who made them. A tile doesn't depend on any one site staying up or staying friendly. Anything that understands web tiles can open it, and its maker can take it with them. The idea comes from Robin Berjon and the DASL project. The Foundry gives you an easy way to make your own.

What signing in allows

Signing in allows foundry.thunderbird.cafe to publish Web Tile changes to your repo. The Foundry doesn't touch your posts, likes, follows or anything else. Your tiles live in your account, not on the Foundry, and they're yours to keep or delete.
